# Stellar Rampage

**A fast-paced, endless space survival shooter where your only way to move is to shoot.**

Stranded in deep space, you fight through escalating waves of enemies with nothing but a recoil-driven blaster and a limited booster pack. There's no finish line, just your next high score.

## Overview

Stellar Rampage is a score-driven arena survival game built around a single twist: **movement and combat are the same action.** Every shot from your blaster propels you in the opposite direction, so positioning is a constant risk-reward decision. Clear the enemies in front of you, but don't recoil straight into the pack behind.

The game is endless by design. Survive, level up, and unlock upgrades to push your run further than the last.

## Gameplay Features

- **Recoil-based movement** — Your blaster is both your weapon and your engine. Bullets fire in one direction and push you in the other, making every engagement a spatial puzzle.
- **Limited booster pack** — A secondary thruster with a finite fuel supply that regenerates over time, giving you controlled bursts of movement when the blaster isn't enough.
- **Level-up upgrade system** — Each level grants a choice of upgrades, letting you shape a different build every run.
- **Varied enemies and upgrades** — Multiple enemy types and upgrade paths keep encounters and progression fresh.
